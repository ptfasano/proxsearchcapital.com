Strategy

Minority equity, sponsor-led.

PROX Search Capital takes minority, non-control equity positions in US-based lower middle market businesses. Every investment is led by an independent sponsor or an experienced acquisition entrepreneur who brings the deal, the operator, and meaningful capital of their own.

We are industry agnostic. We size each check to the deal rather than spreading capital evenly.

  • Established, profitable businesses. No startups or turnarounds
  • Minority positions, sponsor and management retain control
  • US based, lower 48
  • Sponsor capital alongside ours in every deal
Why This Market

Small enough to be overlooked. Established enough to be durable.

Underserved by institutional capital

Businesses at $1M to $5M of EBITDA sit below most private equity mandates and above most individual buyers, which keeps entry pricing rational.

Operators with real alignment

Independent sponsors invest their own capital and their own time. We invest behind people whose outcome depends on the same things ours does.

Diligence from operators

Our team has run businesses, managed integrations, and closed transactions. We underwrite what we have lived, not just what the model shows.
