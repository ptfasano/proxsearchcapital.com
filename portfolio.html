How we size a position.

We do not write the same check into every deal. We size each investment to the opportunity depending on size, risk, growth potential and portfolio overlap.

  • Sizing

    Sized to the deal

    Company size, risk profile, and growth potential set the check.

  • Conviction

    Anchor positions

    A few investments per fund where we take a larger position and a more involved role: a board seat, regular advising, and closer contact with the sponsor and operator.

  • Spread

    Diversified by industry

    We are industry agnostic and build across sectors, which keeps the portfolio from concentrating in any single end market.
