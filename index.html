What We Invest In

What a fit looks like.

We seek highly-motivated sponsors who are focused on acquiring stable, profitable lower middle market businesses with strong margins and clear growth potential.

See the full criteria

Business profile

  • History of operations and profitability
  • Stable and recurring revenue
  • Seller has a valid reason to exit, such as retirement
  • Adjusted EBITDA of $1M–$5M
  • Stable or growing industries
  • Limited customer concentration
  • Opportunities to create value and growth
  • Industry agnostic: business services, specialty manufacturing, consumer and home services are common examples

Sponsor profile

  • Highly motivated and hands-on
  • Strong strategy for value creation
  • Relevant operating, investing, or transaction experience
  • Excellent communication and management skills
  • Meaningful personal investment in the transaction
  • Thoughtful and balanced economics
