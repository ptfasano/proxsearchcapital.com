For Sponsors

A capital partner who understands the deal.

We work with independent sponsors as minority equity partners in lower middle market acquisitions. We understand the complexities of getting a transaction from LOI to close, and we aim to be a thoughtful, responsive capital partner through underwriting and beyond, bringing our own substantial transaction and operations experience.

How We Work

Every deal is different. Our approach reflects that.

Sponsors do not need another investor who takes six weeks to say maybe. Here is what you get from us instead.

  • 01  Straightforward

    We tell you where we stand

    We communicate clearly about what we like, what concerns us, and where we are in our own process.

  • 02  Flexible

    We read each deal on its merits

    Capital structures, sponsor roles, and transaction dynamics vary from deal to deal. We do not force yours into a template.

  • 03  Responsive

    We keep pace with the transaction

    Sponsors need capital partners who can move with a deal. We prioritize efficient underwriting and clear communication throughout.

  • 04  Partner, not operator

    You run the business

    We back sponsors and management teams. We bring an investor's perspective, transaction experience, and support when useful, without trying to run the company.

How We Underwrite

We underwrite the base case.

We are especially excited where the sponsor's base case is built on historical performance, the same multiple, the same growth, the same margins, and assumes a transition period with minimal growth.

Upside is welcome. We just do not want to pay for it before it exists.

Sponsor Profile

What we look for in a sponsor.

We don't believe there is one formula for a great independent sponsor. We back experienced sponsors with relevant operating, investing, or transaction experience, meaningful alignment, and a clear plan for creating value.

  • Experienced

    You have done this before

    Demonstrated leadership, operating, investing, or transaction experience relevant to the opportunity.

  • Committed

    Your own capital is in

    Meaningful personal investment in the transaction and a clear commitment to the business beyond closing.

  • Aligned

    The structure works for everyone

    Thoughtful sponsor economics and a structure that creates strong alignment among the sponsor, investors, and management team.

  • Value-Add

    You know how this gets better

    A clear thesis for the investment and an ability to create value through operational improvement, growth, or strategic execution.

Where the sponsor is not running the business day to day, we will want to meet and vet the operator. The operator should hold meaningful equity and have real skin in the game.

Business Criteria

The business itself.

  • History of operations and profitability
  • Stable and recurring revenue
  • Seller has a valid reason to exit, such as retirement
  • Adjusted EBITDA of $1M–$5M
  • Stable or growing industries
  • Limited customer concentration
  • Opportunities to create value and growth
  • Industry agnostic: business services, specialty manufacturing, consumer and home services are common examples
After the Close

We are not in your inbox every week.

We do not require a board seat, and we are not looking to sit in your management meeting. You run the business.

On our anchor positions, the deals we are most excited about, we are more involved: a board seat, regular advising, and more frequent contact with both the sponsor and the operator. We will tell you upfront which kind of investment we think this is.

Not a Fit

Where we will pass.

Four filters that save everyone time. If your deal hits one of these, we are not the right capital, and we would rather you know now.

  • Heavy customer concentration. A business that leans on one or two accounts for most of its revenue.
  • Operating positions. We invest as a minority partner. We are not the operators.
  • Outside the lower 48. US based only.
  • No history of profit. Startups, turnarounds, and pre-profit businesses are outside what we underwrite.
